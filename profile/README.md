## 🚀 About CityLegs
**CityLegs** ([citylegs.in](https://citylegs.in)) is a two-sided real-world task marketplace. Crucially, we are **NOT** a courier or food delivery app. While couriers move boxes from Point A to Point B, CityLegs provides verified human eyes, local judgment, and physical on-ground presence in distant cities.

We deliver geotagged photographs, structured checklists, timestamps, stamped receipts, and conclusive proof of execution. Our platform operates across **6 major Indian hub cities**: Mumbai (and 125+ nearby towns), Pune, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Rajkot, and Surat.

## 🧠 Core Engineering & AI Architecture
CityLegs operates on the principle: *"Automate Normal Work • Human Review for Exceptions."* Our backend is designed to be deterministic, secure, and highly scalable.

### 1. AI-Powered Scope Classification
Clients input tasks in plain natural language. Our AI (integrated with Google Gemini) extracts the core objective, generates a step-by-step execution checklist, and classifies the task into one of 4 standardized levels:
*   **Level 1 (₹499):** Simple Visit & Visual Check (15-45 mins)
*   **Level 2 (₹899):** Document & Submission (30-90 mins)
*   **Level 3 (₹1,299):** Verification & Coordination (1-2.5 hours)
*   **Level 4 (₹1,499):** Complex Field Work & Measurements (2+ hours)

### 2. Deterministic Safety Screening
Every request undergoes automated keyword screening and AI compliance evaluation, categorized into:
*   🟢 **GREEN:** Safe for immediate auto-dispatch.
*   🟡 **YELLOW:** Requires operational spot-check.
*   🔴 **RED:** Prohibited/restricted tasks (contraband, illegal activity) - Blocked.

### 3. 100% Escrow Protection & Payouts
Security is our priority. Payments are held in a neutral escrow account. **The Runner receives their payout ONLY after the client reviews the geotagged proof, checklist, and stamps, and clicks 'Approve'.** 

### 4. Intelligent Dispatch Engine
Our matching algorithm assigns verified Runners based on geohash proximity, category experience, active workload, and their internal Trust Score. The execution state machine tracks: `Travel (En Route) → Arrived → In Progress → Proof Uploaded`.
